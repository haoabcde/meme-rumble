# 奶蛙与水鲸 V3

## 本轮规则

- 奶蛙普通技能1和大招使用已有的 `posture:any` 接口，允许地面/空中发动。上技能1仍沿用地面版。空中施法正常下落；大招演出期间连下落速度、位置和所有计时一起暂停，演出结束后恢复。
- 强化15秒：普攻和技能伤害仍为1.5倍；普通技能1半径从1.5倍改为2倍，即480单位/6个身位；其他普攻范围仍为1.5倍，全地图技能仍覆盖整张地图。地面移动和空中横移速度1.5倍，实际承受伤害0.7倍，不改变受击框、击退、格挡承伤上限、冲刺时长或跳跃预算。
- 变身圣光以人物当下坐标为中心，高度乘2，宽度和完整播放时间保留；羽毛一同跟随。强化出招始终使用神圣常态姿势；移动则有独立的悬浮循环。
- 普通二技能鲸鱼每24个可推进帧一圈，独立于地图宽度和风柱远行寿命。防反/定点二技能的48帧鲸影与滞后节奏保持原样。
- 水潭及所有水鲸在运行时按亮度映射为淡蓝色，保留透明水体、高光和明暗。没有改动原始水鲸图集。
- 梁文峰居中，移除手画直线爆发，改用库内 mana-burst、thunderclap、wave-crash；全部隐藏库内演示人物，配蓝白色。演出仍60帧，战场时停。
- 按用户确认采用三次主跃出：第1/5/9段分别出大鲸，逐次增大、增高；快速出水、跃顶稍缓、加速入水，尾部随身躯形变，增加池边喷溅与落水环浪。其他段由水鱼/水花穿插。预警仍1秒，12段各35、合计420伤害；装饰不增加判定。

## 生图提示词与方式

使用内置 imagegen，透明背景；生成结果复制进项目，原始文件保留。图片仅新增移动动作和符文，不覆盖已定稿图集。

### 移动图集

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent pixel-art game sprite sheet, EXACTLY 4 columns x 2 rows, 8 equal 384x384 cells, 1536x768 canvas. Input image 1 is character identity and pixel art style reference: use its FIRST idle yellow plump frog with cream oval belly, olive hands/feet, small round green eyes, tiny flat mouth, short thick legs, exact same proportions and face. Input image 2 is angel identity reference, use TOP LEFT sacred closed-eye relaxed-arms pose. Produce NEW MOVEMENT POSES only, no attacks. TOP ROW: four successive phases of a looping walk/run toward screen right: left foot forward right leg back; passing legs left knee lifted; right foot forward left leg back; passing legs right knee lifted. Arms swing opposite legs, belly weight shifts naturally, face remains the same. It must visibly alternate the two legs, no duplicated rigid idle. BOTTOM ROW: four subtle hover movement phases for the SAME white-and-gold robed angel frog, closed eyes serene face, arms relaxed as reference, slight forward drift/lean, wings gently flex, robe hem streaming gently left. Do not turn the angel into an attack. All eight sprites face right 3/4 view, consistent ground baseline and centered torso per cell. Preserve established sprite identities, body volumes, colors and hard pixel edges; no redesign, no text, no lines/grid, no glow clouds, no background scenery, no drop shadows, no frame numbers, no cut off toes/wings. Fully transparent background and generous space separating all cells, crisp pixel art, game-ready.

### 参考特效改绘

Use case: style-transfer and precise-object-edit. Make a production sprite atlas of FOUR isolated gold holy magic VFX motifs selected from the reference, transformed into crisp low-resolution PIXEL ART consistent with a 2D pixel fighting game. 2 columns x 2 rows in equal square cells, 1024x1024, transparent background, no grid. Top left: the reference's sharp asymmetric starburst impact (second row left), a white-hot core and tapered golden rays with tiny gold sparks, NO opaque background. Top right: reference third row left concentric rune circle, thin gold lines, layered geometric rings, a few stylized unreadable rune glyphs, CENTER EMPTY and fully transparent, front-on round disk. Bottom left: reference lower left spiral ribbons winding upward in a soft helix, few rune marks, white-gold highlights, distinct separate ribbon strands, transparent gaps. Bottom right: reference bottom middle eight-point holy star with two orbit rings, elegant gold and cream, transparent gaps. Preserve the reference designs' elegance and line rhythm but redraw hard-edged coherent PIXEL clusters with limited gold/cream/white palette, NO antialiasing, no realistic smoke/blur, no black rectangle, no characters, no words, no watermarks. All four centered in their own cells with generous transparent margins and no cross-cell overlap. These are overlay sprites; leave real alpha transparency.
